## 1. Brand Overview

### Mission Statement

We make modern apparel that works as hard as you do. BIRD THREE exists at the intersection of functional fabrics, sleek minimalist design, and artist collaboration — creating clothes that perform, endure, and mean something.

### Brand Values

**Functional First**
Every piece begins with its purpose. We select fabrics for what they do — regulate temperature, resist water, stretch with movement, breathe under pressure — then design around that performance. Function is not a feature. It is the foundation.

**Sleek Design**
We believe in the power of restraint. Clean lines. Considered details. Nothing decorative, nothing wasted. Our silhouettes are minimal by conviction, not by default. Every seam, every cut, every proportion is deliberate.

**Art-Driven**
Clothing is a canvas. Each season we partner with emerging artists to embed original work into the fabric of everyday wear — hand-drawn illustrations, abstract compositions, graphic explorations. Limited runs. Permanent impact.

**Built to Last**
We reject disposability. Our materials are chosen for longevity. Our construction is built to endure seasons, not trends. We would rather you own four pieces you wear for years than forty you discard in months.

### Brand Personality

BIRD THREE speaks with quiet confidence. We are not loud, not flashy, not desperate for attention. We are the person in the room who does not need to explain themselves — the work speaks.

| Trait | We Are | We Are Not |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Calm, direct, assured | Aggressive, hyperbolic, salesy |
| Register | Intelligent but accessible | Academic or pretentious |
| Energy | Steady, grounded | Frantic, trend-chasing |
| Warmth | Welcoming but not eager | Cold or exclusionary |
| Humor | Dry, occasional, understated | Jokey, meme-driven, sarcastic |

Our voice sounds like a knowledgeable friend who happens to know a great deal about textiles, design, and contemporary art — and shares that knowledge without condescension.

### Target Audience

**Primary: The Considered Consumer**
Ages 26-42. Urban and semi-urban. Design-literate. They work in creative industries, technology, architecture, or adjacent fields. They have moved past fast fashion but are not interested in traditional luxury signaling. They want clothes that function in their actual lives — commuting, working, traveling, going out — without compromising on aesthetic standards. They notice materials. They appreciate restraint. They buy less and keep longer.

**Secondary: The Art Collector Crossover**
Ages 28-50. They follow emerging artists. They attend gallery openings. They see clothing as an extension of personal curation. The artist collaboration pieces speak directly to them — wearable editions that sit at the intersection of fashion and contemporary art.

## 2. Visual Identity

### Logo

The BIRD THREE wordmark is the primary brand identifier. It is typeset in Inter at 600 weight with 0.2em letter-spacing, rendered in uppercase.

**Specifications:**

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Typeface | Inter |
| Weight | 600 (Semi Bold) |
| Letter-spacing | 0.2em |
| Case | Uppercase |
| Reference size | 1.125rem (18px at base) |
| Primary color | Off-Black #1A1A1A |
| Reversed color | Warm-White #FAF9F7 |

**Usage Rules:**

- The wordmark must always appear in Inter 600. Never substitute another typeface.
- Minimum clear space around the wordmark equals the cap-height of the "B" on all four sides.
- Never rotate, distort, add effects, outlines, or shadows to the wordmark.
- The wordmark appears in only two color treatments: Off-Black on light backgrounds, or Warm-White on dark backgrounds. Never use accent colors for the wordmark.
- Never set the wordmark in lowercase, title case, or mixed case. It is always BIRD THREE in full uppercase.
- The space between "BIRD" and "THREE" is a standard word space at 0.2em tracking. Do not manually kern or tighten.
- Minimum display size: 12px (digital), 8mm cap-height (print).

#### Usage Ratios

- **70% Neutrals** — Warm-White, Off-White, and Silver dominate backgrounds and negative space.
- **25% Dark Tones** — Off-Black, Charcoal, and Dark Gray carry text, buttons, and structural elements.
- **5% Warm Accents** — Stone, Sand, and Clay appear sparingly as intentional moments of warmth.

#### Color Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Use Off-Black (#1A1A1A) as the default text color, not pure Black (#0A0A0A).
- Use Warm-White (#FAF9F7) as the default page background, not pure White (#FFFFFF).
- Use Dark Gray (#4A4A4A) for body paragraph text to soften readability.
- Alternate section backgrounds between Warm-White (#FAF9F7) and Off-White (#F5F3F0) to create subtle rhythm.
- Use Stone (#B8A99A) for eyebrow labels and small taxonomic text.
- Reserve Clay (#A0877A) for editorial italic emphasis inside display typography.

**Don't:**
- Never use warm accents (Stone, Sand, Clay) as background fills for large surfaces.
- Never combine two warm accents side by side without a neutral separator.
- Never use functional colors (Success, Error) for decorative purposes.
- Never place Medium Gray text on Off-White backgrounds — contrast is insufficient for accessibility.
- Never introduce colors outside this palette. No blues, no reds (aside from Error), no greens (aside from Success).

### Photography Direction

**Mood:** Quiet confidence. Natural light. Unhurried. The feeling of a well-considered morning or a calm late afternoon. Nothing staged to the point of sterility — there should be a sense of lived-in ease, but always composed.

**Lighting:**
- Natural light is strongly preferred — soft directional daylight, golden hour, overcast diffusion.
- Studio lighting, when necessary, should be soft and directional. Never flat, never harsh.
- Deep, controlled shadows are welcome. They add dimension and mood.

**Color Temperature:**
- Warm neutral. Images should feel at home within our palette — no blue-cast clinical tones, no oversaturated warmth.
- Post-processing should lean toward desaturation. Let the fabric textures and tonal range speak.

**Composition:**
- Generous negative space. Let the garment breathe.
- Favor asymmetric compositions over centered framing.
- Show fabric detail — close crops of texture, drape, stitching.
- Environmental context should be minimal and architectural: concrete, linen, wood, stone, matte surfaces.

**Models:**
- Diverse, real, understated. No exaggerated posing.
- Movement should be natural — walking, turning, adjusting a sleeve. Not performed.
- Expressions: neutral to quietly engaged. No forced smiles. No intensity.

**Product Flat Lays:**
- Shot on Off-White or Warm-White surfaces only.
- Single garment per frame, or a considered pairing of two to three items maximum.
- Zero styling props. The garment is the entire subject.

### Art Direction for Collaborations

Artist collaboration pieces are the expressive counterweight to our minimalist core line. They are permitted — encouraged — to break visual rules that the mainline respects.

**Selection Criteria:**
- We partner with emerging and mid-career artists, not established market names.
- Work should feel raw, human, handmade — even if digitally produced.
- We favor abstract, graphic, and illustrative work. No photorealism. No kitsch.

**Integration:**
- Artwork is embedded into the garment through print, jacquard, or embroidery — never applied as a surface afterthought.
- Each collaboration receives its own visual world for marketing: unique color grading, bespoke photography, and the artist's own visual language honored in layout.

**Marketing Treatment:**
- Collaboration campaigns use a side-by-side layout: artist text/bio on one side, artwork gallery on the other.
- Artist quotes appear as blockquotes: 2px left border in Stone (#B8A99A), Cormorant Garamond italic, 1.25rem, with a cite line in Inter 0.8125rem.
- Eyebrow labels for collaboration sections: "Artist Collaboration" or "Art x Apparel".

## 3. Design Principles

### Spacing System

All spacing is built on an **8px base unit**. Every margin, padding, and gap should be a multiple of 8.

| Token | Value | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| xs | 4px | Tight internal gaps (grid image gutters, inline spacing) |
| sm | 8px | Minimum gap between related elements, artwork grid gaps |
| md | 12px | Eyebrow-to-heading spacing, small internal padding |
| base | 16px | Product card image-to-info gap, standard element spacing |
| lg | 20px | Collection card image-to-info gap, mobile nav link gaps |
| xl | 24px | Standard grid gaps, product grid column gaps, mobile section padding |
| 2xl | 32px | Blockquote top margin, generous internal spacing |
| 3xl | 36px | Newsletter form top margin, button group spacing |
| 4xl | 48px | Section header-to-content gap, navigation link gaps (desktop max) |
| 5xl | 60px | Desktop horizontal padding max, large content gaps |
| 6xl | 80px | Minimum large section vertical padding |
| 7xl | 120px | Standard large section vertical padding max |
| 8xl | 140px | Maximum section vertical padding (brand statement, artwork, collaboration) |

**Responsive spacing** uses CSS `clamp()` to fluidly scale between breakpoints:

```
Section vertical padding: clamp(60px, 8vw, 120px)   — standard sections
                          clamp(80px, 10vw, 140px)   — emphasis sections (brand statement, artwork, newsletter)

Horizontal page padding:  clamp(24px, 4vw, 60px)     — universal

Content gaps:             clamp(40px, 6vw, 80px)      — between text and media in split layouts
```

### Grid System

**Container:**
- Maximum width: 1400px
- Centered with `margin: 0 auto`
- Horizontal padding: `clamp(24px, 4vw, 60px)`

**Page Layouts:**

| Layout | Columns | Gap | Breakpoint Behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | `1fr 1fr` | 0 | Single column below 768px |
| Collections Preview | `1fr 1fr` | 24px | Single column below 768px |
| Product Grid | `repeat(3, 1fr)` | 32px row / 24px column | 2 columns below 992px, 1 column below 480px |
| Featured Grid | `repeat(4, 1fr)` | 24px | 2 columns below 992px, 1 column below 480px |
| Lookbook Grid | `repeat(3, 1fr)` | 8px | 2 columns below 768px, 1 column below 480px |
| Split Content | `1fr 1fr` or `1fr 1.2fr` | `clamp(40px, 6vw, 80px)` | Single column below 768px |
| Artwork Gallery | `1fr 1fr` (2x2, with span) | 8px | Retains grid at all sizes |
| Previous Seasons | `repeat(3, 1fr)` | 24px | Single column below 768px |

**Breakpoints:**

| Name | Value | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | 480px | Single-column product grids, stacked newsletter forms, collapsed lookbook |
| Tablet | 768px | Navigation collapse, split layouts stack, grids reduce columns |
| Desktop | 992px | Product grid reduces from 3 to 2 columns |

### Shape Language

**Border-radius: 0 — everywhere, always.**

This is non-negotiable. Every element in the BIRD THREE system has sharp, square corners. Buttons, inputs, cards, images, containers, overlays, tooltips — all zero radius.

```css
$border-radius:    0;
$border-radius-lg: 0;
$border-radius-sm: 0;
```

This is an intentional design decision. Sharp edges communicate precision, intention, and an architectural sensibility. Rounded corners suggest softness and approachability — qualities that belong to other brands.

**Borders:**
- Dividers between page sections: 1px solid Silver (#E8E8E8)
- Navigation bottom border: 1px solid Light Gray at 50% opacity
- Input fields on dark backgrounds: 1px solid Charcoal (#2D2D2D), transitioning to Medium Gray on focus
- Blockquote left accent: 2px solid Stone (#B8A99A)
- Text link underlines: 1px solid Off-Black (#1A1A1A)
- Collection card labels: no border, background rgba(#1A1A1A, 0.7), padding 8px 16px

### Motion & Animation

All motion in the BIRD THREE system is **subtle, functional, and ease-based**. Animation exists to provide feedback and smooth transitions — never to entertain or impress.

**Timing Functions:**
- Default easing: `ease` (CSS ease keyword)
- Never use `linear` for UI transitions
- Never use bounce or elastic easing

**Duration Scale:**

| Duration | Usage |
|---|---|
| 0.2s | Micro-interactions: link color changes, border-color focus states, general hover states |
| 0.3s | Standard transitions: button hover fills, navigation underline reveals, navbar state changes |
| 0.4s | Content transitions: lookbook caption fade-in, product card scale transforms |
| 0.5s | Larger element transitions: collection card image scale on hover |
| 0.6s | Image transforms: lookbook image zoom on hover |

**Specific Animations:**

Navigation link underline:
```css
/* Underline grows from left on hover */
width: 0 → 100%
height: 1px
background-color: #1A1A1A
transition: width 0.3s ease
```

Button hover (primary):
```css
/* Inverts fill on hover */
background-color: #1A1A1A → #FAF9F7
color: #FAF9F7 → #1A1A1A
transition: all 0.3s ease
```

Product card image hover:
```css
/* Subtle scale-down for a press-like feel */
transform: scale(1) → scale(0.98)
transition: transform 0.4s ease
```

Lookbook image hover:
```css
/* Subtle scale-up to draw attention */
transform: scale(1) → scale(1.03)
transition: transform 0.6s ease
```

Lookbook caption reveal:
```css
/* Fades up from behind gradient overlay */
opacity: 0 → 1
transform: translateY(8px) → translateY(0)
transition: all 0.4s ease
```

**Motion Don'ts:**
- No bouncing, springing, or overshooting animations.
- No loading spinners with brand colors. Use a simple pulsing opacity on neutral gray.
- No parallax scrolling effects.
- No auto-playing carousels or sliders.
- No transitions longer than 0.6s for any UI element.
- No animation on page load other than standard browser rendering. Elements should be in their final state immediately.

### Iconography

**Style:**
- Line-based, 1.5px stroke weight (matching the hamburger menu bar thickness)
- No fills. Outlines only.
- Geometric construction — straight lines and simple curves
- Square proportions on a 24px x 24px grid
- Color: Off-Black (#1A1A1A) default, Dark Gray (#4A4A4A) for secondary/muted states

**Menu Icon (Hamburger):**
- Three horizontal bars, each 24px wide and 1.5px tall
- 6px vertical gap between bars
- Color: Off-Black (#1A1A1A)

**Principles:**
- Icons should be immediately legible at 24px. If an icon requires study, it is too complex.
- Never use filled/solid icon variants.
- Never use decorative or illustrative icons. Every icon must have a clear functional purpose.
- Maintain consistent stroke weight across the entire icon set.

## 4. Brand Voice & Copy

### How We Write

BIRD THREE copy is **concise, specific, and confident**. We state facts. We describe materials, construction, and purpose. We do not beg, hype, or oversell.

Every word should earn its place. If a sentence works without an adjective, remove the adjective.

### Headline Style

Headlines are short, declarative, and often structured around tension or contrast.

**Examples:**

```
Form Follows Function
Wearable Art
Stay in the Loop
Men's Essentials
Women's Essentials
```

**Patterns:**
- Three to five words maximum for hero headlines.
- No exclamation marks. Ever.
- Title case for display headlines (h1, h2).
- Sentence case is acceptable for conversational contexts (newsletter, social).
- Em dashes (—) are preferred over hyphens for pause and separation.
- Ampersands (&) may replace "and" in display contexts for visual brevity.

### Eyebrow Labels

The small tracked-out labels that sit above headlines. They provide context, category, and temporal grounding.

**Examples:**

```
Modern Apparel — SS26
Art × Apparel
Artist Collaboration
Functional Fabrics
```

**Rules:**
- Always uppercase.
- Always use the multiplication sign (×) not the letter "x" when joining concepts.
- Season codes use standard abbreviations: SS (Spring/Summer), FW (Fall/Winter) followed by two-digit year.

### Product Descriptions

Product copy leads with the fabric or technology, follows with the fit or construction detail.

**Structure:** Material/technology + finish/hand + fit descriptor

**Examples:**

```
Water-resistant ripstop · Relaxed fit
Temperature regulating · Unisex
4-way stretch · Tapered leg
Organic cotton · Limited edition
```

**Rules:**
- Use the interpunct (·) to separate attributes, not pipes, commas, or slashes.
- Lead with the functional property of the fabric, not the aesthetic.
- Keep to two attributes per product card. Save longer descriptions for the product detail page.
- Fabric descriptions are specific and technical: say "water-resistant ripstop" not "weatherproof." Say "4-way stretch" not "flexible."
- On the product detail page, expand with one to two sentences of plain, informative prose. No marketing language. Describe the garment as if you were explaining it to someone who genuinely wants to understand what they are buying.

**Product Detail Page Example:**

> **Technical Overshirt**
> $185
>
> Cut from a Japanese water-resistant ripstop with a soft hand and matte finish. Relaxed through the body with articulated sleeves for unrestricted movement. Two chest pockets with concealed snap closures. Unlined for layering versatility across three seasons.

### Social Media Voice

Social copy is slightly warmer and more conversational than website copy, but retains the same restraint and specificity.

**Principles:**
- Speak to one person, not an audience.
- Show the product or the process. Do not describe a lifestyle.
- Captions are one to three sentences. Shorter is better.
- Let the image carry the weight. The caption provides context, not narration.
- Hashtags: three maximum per post, placed at the end, brand-relevant only.

**Examples:**

```
New drop. Technical Overshirt in Japanese ripstop. Water-resistant, three-season weight.

SS26 collaboration with painter Mara Kessler. Four pieces. Hand-drawn linework on organic cotton.

The Stretch Tailored Trouser — 4-way stretch, tapered leg, zero compromise.
```

**Social Don'ts:**
- Never use "link in bio" as a CTA in the caption body.
- Never post user-generated content without explicit permission and credit.
- Never use trending audio or memes unless they genuinely align with brand tone.
- Never use all-caps for emphasis. Let the words work at normal volume.

### Newsletter Copy

The newsletter is a direct line to our most engaged audience. Tone is warm, informative, and respectful of their attention.

**Subject line style:** Short, specific, no clickbait. Examples:
- "SS26 is here"
- "New collaboration: Mara Kessler"
- "Restocked: Technical Overshirt"

**Body copy:** Leads with the news, follows with one to two lines of context, ends with a single clear CTA.

**Signup prompt example:**
> New drops, artist collaborations, and early access. No spam, ever.

## 5. Application Examples

### Website Layout Principles

**Page Structure:**
- Fixed navigation bar at top: 72px height, Warm-White at 95% opacity with 12px backdrop blur
- Content begins below the navbar (72px offset)
- Sections alternate between Warm-White (#FAF9F7) and Off-White (#F5F3F0) backgrounds to create visual rhythm
- Dark sections (newsletter, select features) use Off-Black (#1A1A1A) background with Warm-White text
- All content containers: max-width 1400px, centered, with `clamp(24px, 4vw, 60px)` horizontal padding
- Footer follows the same container pattern

**Section Anatomy:**

```
[Section]
  padding: clamp(60px, 8vw, 120px) 0
  background: alternating $warm-white / $off-white

  [Inner Container]
    max-width: 1400px
    margin: 0 auto
    padding: 0 clamp(24px, 4vw, 60px)

    [Section Header]
      margin-bottom: 48px
      Optional: flex row with h2 and "View All" link at baseline

    [Content Grid]
      gap: 24px (standard) or 8px (tight/image)
```

**Text Selection:**
```css
::selection {
  background-color: #1A1A1A;
  color: #FAF9F7;
}
```

**Scroll behavior:** `scroll-behavior: smooth` on the html element.

### Product Card Treatment

The product card is one of the most repeated components. Consistency is critical.

**Structure:**

```
[Product Card]
  display: flex
  flex-direction: column

  [Image Container]
    aspect-ratio: 3 / 4
    background-color: #E8E8E8 (Silver, fallback)
    overflow: hidden
    margin-bottom: 16px
    Hover: scale(0.98) over 0.4s ease

  [Info Block]
    [Product Name — h4]
      font-size: 0.9375rem
      font-weight: 500
      color: #1A1A1A
      margin-bottom: 4px

    [Detail Line]
      font-size: 0.8125rem
      color: #8A8A8A
      margin-bottom: 8px
      Format: "Attribute · Attribute"

    [Price]
      font-size: 0.875rem
      font-weight: 500
      color: #1A1A1A
```

**Grid Placement:**
- Default: 3 columns at 24px column gap, 32px row gap
- Featured: 4 columns at 24px gap
- The product image has no border-radius, no border, no shadow
- On hover, the image scales to 0.98 (a subtle press-in effect), not a zoom-in

### Editorial / Lookbook Layout

The lookbook is the most expressive page in the system. It carries more visual density and editorial ambition.

**Grid:**
- 3-column masonry-like grid with 8px gaps (not 24px — tight gaps create a denser, gallery-like feel)
- Items can span 2 rows (tall) or 2 columns (wide) for visual variety
- Minimum image height: 320px

**Hover Behavior:**
- Image scales up to 1.03 over 0.6s ease (the opposite of product cards, which scale down)
- Caption overlay fades in from below: gradient from transparent to rgba(#1A1A1A, 0.85)
- Caption includes a Cormorant Garamond number/index (1.5rem, weight 300, Stone color) and a description line (0.8125rem, Silver color)

**Season Sections:**
- Each season gets a header block: max-width 700px, h2 + paragraph description in Medium Gray
- Spacing below header: `clamp(40px, 6vw, 64px)`

**Artist Collaboration Block:**
- Split layout: text content left, 2x2 artwork gallery right
- Artwork tiles are square (aspect-ratio: 1), 8px gap
- Blockquote with 2px Stone left border and 24px left padding
- Artist attribution in Inter 0.8125rem, Medium Gray, 0.04em tracking

### Email Newsletter Style

**Layout:**
- Single column, 560px maximum width (matching website newsletter container)
- Warm-White (#FAF9F7) background
- Off-Black (#1A1A1A) text

**Header:**

### Social Media Post Guidelines

**Image Specifications:**
- Instagram feed: 1:1 (1080x1080px) or 4:5 (1080x1350px)
- Instagram Stories: 9:16 (1080x1920px)
- All other platforms: 16:9 (1920x1080px) or 1:1

**Image Treatment:**
- Photography follows brand photography direction (see Section 2)
- Warm neutral color grading, never oversaturated
- Text overlays, when used, are set in Inter 500 or 600, uppercase, 0.1em-0.2em tracking, in Warm-White or Off-Black depending on image brightness
- Text overlays must have sufficient contrast. When over photography, use a subtle dark gradient or solid color block behind text

**Post Types:**

*Product Launch:*
- Lead image: product shot on model or flat lay
- Caption: product name, one material/fit detail, price
- No more than three hashtags

*Artist Collaboration:*
- Lead image: collaboration artwork or product featuring the art
- Caption: artist name, collaboration title, brief description
- Tag the artist

*Lookbook / Editorial:*
- Lead image: strongest editorial shot from the series
- Caption: season name, one evocative line, link direction
- Carousel of three to five additional images

*Behind the Scenes:*
- More casual framing is permitted, but maintain brand color temperature
- Caption: brief, factual context about what is being shown

**Grid Aesthetic (Instagram):**
- Alternate between close-up detail shots, full outfit/product shots, and artwork/texture images
- Maintain a consistent warm-neutral tone across the grid
- Avoid back-to-back posts with identical compositions

### Packaging Direction

**Materials:**
- Uncoated, unbleached stock in a warm off-white tone matching our Warm-White (#FAF9F7) as closely as the medium allows
- Matte finish only. No gloss, no foil, no spot UV unless for a limited artist collaboration edition
- Tissue paper: unbleached, unprinted

**Printing:**
- Wordmark and essential text printed in a warm dark tone matching Off-Black (#1A1A1A)
- Single-color printing only for mainline products
- Artist collaboration packaging may introduce the collaboration's accent color as a second print color

**Box / Mailer:**
- Clean, minimal construction. No excessive structural design
- BIRD THREE wordmark centered on the top face or front panel
- Interior: clean, unprinted, or a single line of brand text printed small on the interior flap
- Sizing and care information on a separate card, not printed on the box

**Garment Tags:**
- Woven label: Off-Black fabric, Warm-White text, BIRD THREE wordmark
- Hang tag: Warm-White card stock, Off-Black text, wordmark on front, product details and care on reverse
- Shape: rectangular, sharp corners (matching zero-radius principle)

**Shipping:**
- Outer mailer: recyclable corrugated, unbleached or kraft
- Interior protection: tissue wrap, no plastic
- Sticker seal: circular, 38mm diameter, wordmark in Off-Black on Warm-White stock, matte finish
