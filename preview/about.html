Our Story

Born from a belief that clothes should work as hard as you do — and look effortless doing it.

Where It Started

Bird Three began with a simple frustration: why choose between looking good and feeling good? Founded by a couple who couldn't find clothes that matched their lifestyle — functional enough for everyday movement, designed with the care of something you'd actually want to wear.

So they started making their own. What began as personal projects in a small studio grew into a brand built on one principle: form follows function, but never at the expense of beauty.

What We Stand For

01

Functional First

Every fabric is chosen for its performance properties — moisture-wicking, temperature-regulating, four-way stretch, water-resistant. Your clothes should keep up with your life.

02

Sleek by Design

Clean lines, considered proportions, zero excess. We strip away the unnecessary to let the materials and construction speak. Modern pieces that fit into any wardrobe, any context.

03

Art Inside

We collaborate with artists every season to bring unique prints, graphics, and details into our collections. Fashion should inspire — and every piece should feel like it has a soul.

04

Made to Last

We don't do trends. We design pieces you'll reach for years from now. Quality construction, timeless design, and fabrics that age gracefully. Buy less, choose well.

The Process

We work directly with specialist mills in Japan, Portugal, and Italy, sourcing technical fabrics that are normally reserved for outdoor or performance gear. Then we cut them into clean, modern silhouettes that feel at home in the city.

Our design process is iterative — prototyping, wearing, refining. Every piece goes through dozens of iterations before it earns the Bird Three label. If it doesn't feel right on the body, it doesn't ship.
