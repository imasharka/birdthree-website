Artist Collaboration

Kaia Mori × Bird Three

For SS26, Tokyo-based artist Kaia Mori brings her signature blend of organic linework and geometric abstraction to our graphic series. Each piece features original artwork created exclusively for this collection — exploring themes of flow, tension, and natural rhythm.

“I wanted the art to feel like it belongs on the fabric — not printed on, but woven into the identity of the garment.” — Kaia Mori
